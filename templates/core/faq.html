Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking entertainment for your event.

Pricing & Quotes

How does guide pricing work?

The price shown on an artist's profile is a starting estimate based on their home region. Once you submit your exact venue location, performance times, and event date, we provide an accurate, all-inclusive quote incorporating travel and scheduling.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. Your final quote includes travel, performance fees, equipment, and insurance. The price we confirm is the exact total you pay.

Booking & Payments

How do I lock in an artist for my date?

Submit an enquiry through the artist's profile. Once we confirm their availability for your date and location, you can secure the booking by paying a deposit and signing a digital contract.

What are the payment terms?

A deposit of 20% secures your date. The remaining balance is due prior to the event date via secure online bank transfer.

Event Day & Technical Logistics

Does the act provide their own sound and lighting equipment?

Yes. Unless stated otherwise, all our acts arrive fully self-contained with professional-grade PA systems, sound controls, and stage lighting suitable for standard event spaces.

What requirements does the artist need from my venue?

Acts generally require standard electrical sockets, a clear, flat performance space, access to setup 60-90 minutes before performing, and standard parking access.

Safety & Peace of Mind

What happens if the act falls ill or can't perform?

Every booking is backed by our emergency contract protection. If an artist cannot perform due to unforeseen circumstances, our agency immediately coordinates a vetted replacement act of matching style and standard.

Are your acts insured, and do they have PAT testing?

Yes. All rostered artists hold Public Liability Insurance (PLI) and up-to-date Portable Appliance Testing (PAT) certificates, which are standard requirements for most UK venues.
